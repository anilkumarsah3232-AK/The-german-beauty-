BMW History

A journey of engineering and performance

1916 — The Beginning

BMW traces its origins to 1916 and initially became known for producing aircraft engines.

1923 — BMW Motorcycles

BMW entered motorcycle production and developed motorcycles that became famous for engineering quality.

1928 — BMW Cars

BMW entered automobile manufacturing and gradually established itself as a premium automotive brand.

1972 — BMW M

BMW Motorsport GmbH was created, eventually becoming the legendary BMW M performance division.

Today — M4 & M5

Modern BMW M4 and M5 models combine advanced technology, luxury, everyday usability and high-performance engineering.
